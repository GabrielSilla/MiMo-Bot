mGBA (mgba_libretro.dll)
========================

Peemo Sender bundles the mGBA emulator core, built for the libretro API, to
run Game Boy Advance games on the PC and show them on Peemo's screen.

  License text: https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/
  Source code:  https://github.com/mgba-emu/mgba
  libretro build: https://github.com/libretro/mgba

The library is used unmodified, as a separate DLL that Peemo Sender loads at
run time (cores\mgba_libretro.dll). No game ROMs are included or distributed:
you provide your own.
