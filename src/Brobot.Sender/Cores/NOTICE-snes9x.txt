Snes9x (snes9x_libretro.dll)
============================

Peemo Sender bundles the Snes9x emulator core, built for the libretro API, to
run Super Nintendo games on the PC and show them on Peemo's screen.

The library is used unmodified, as a separate DLL that Peemo Sender loads at
run time (cores\snes9x_libretro.dll). No game ROMs are included or
distributed: you provide your own.

Peemo Sender itself must therefore not be sold or otherwise distributed
commercially while it bundles this core.
